Advisory Services

Through our Investment Advisor platform, we operate with a fiduciary duty to integrate education, analysis, tax strategies, estate planning, and deal-structure consultation for high-net-worth real estate investors on a fee-for-service basis. This model is designed to ensure objectivity and alignment with clients' interests.

Specializing in passive real estate investment programs, we bring decades of expertise and tailored solutions to an asset class that often serves as a cornerstone of affluent investors' portfolios. Our specialized knowledge enables us to address the unique complexities and opportunities within the real estate sector, empowering our clients to make informed, strategic decisions.

Who Qualifies?

Based on our fee structure, the Advisory Services Platform may be more suitable for:

  • Transactions in excess of ten million dollars
  • Complex ownership structures
  • Advanced estate-planning situations
